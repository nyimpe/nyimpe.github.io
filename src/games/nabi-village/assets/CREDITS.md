# Assets

Sprites, UI, effects, sounds and music come from the Ninja Adventure Asset Pack by Pixel-boy and AAA,
released under CC0 1.0 (https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack).
toad.png and toad-face.png are recoloured from the pack's frog. bg-battle.png and bg-map.png are
composed from the pack's nature tileset by scripts/build-nabi-village-assets.py.
