Tetris Classic

2026.06.01

Stack blocks to complete horizontal lines. Clear lines to earn points and keep up as the blocks fall faster.

Tap or press Space to begin. Use ← → to move, ↑ to rotate, ↓ to soft drop, and Space to hard drop. Touch controls are also available below the game.
