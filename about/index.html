About

This is my little corner of the web: a place to make things, try ideas, and keep notes along the way.

For now, it is home to a few small browser games. Over time, I plan to add experiments and short notes about things I find interesting.

Have a look around

Pick a game from the home page. Each page includes a short introduction, controls, and a button to start playing.

To use keyboard controls, click the game screen or focus it with Tab. Outside the game, you can scroll and browse as usual.

You can find the source on GitHub.
